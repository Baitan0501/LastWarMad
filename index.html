How to roast a duck

📋 Step-by-Step Recipe:

  1. Leave only **1 squad** at your base, and send all the rest to the nearest mines or to a friend's alliance house.
  2. The squad remaining at your base should consist of **only one hero** and preferably **1 soldier** (you can quickly heal 1 soldier from the hospital).
  3. Attack the "duck" with your 1-hero squad and check the battle reports to see how many soldiers are actually at the enemy's base. This will show you their **true strength**, not the hidden numbers shown during normal scouting!
  4. If the duck's squad power is low, it means they have few soldiers left and it's safe to attack. However, the duck is still powerful, so make sure to strike back using your **strongest squad**!
